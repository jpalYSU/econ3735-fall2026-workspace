# Screening rubric: Junior Sales Analyst

Score each applicant on the four criteria below, using only the information in the application.

| Criterion | Points | What earns full points |
|---|---|---|
| Excel skills (required) | 30 | Evidence of PivotTables, lookups, and charts in a job, internship, course, or project |
| Written communication (required) | 20 | A clear, specific cover letter excerpt |
| Sales or retail data experience (preferred) | 30 | Hands-on work with sales, retail, or POS data |
| Technical extras (preferred) | 20 | SQL, Python, or Power BI used in real work or projects |

Applicants who can't start by January 5, 2027 don't meet a requirement; list them separately.

Do not use, or let any tool use: name, age or graduation year, address or ZIP code, gender, race, disability,
or any other protected characteristic. Do not penalize career gaps or non-traditional education paths.
A degree is preferred, not required; it is not a scoring criterion.
