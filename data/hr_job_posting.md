# Junior Sales Analyst, Cosmic Crunch (Youngstown, Ohio)

Cosmic Crunch is hiring a Junior Sales Analyst to support the regional sales team.

## What you'll do
- Build weekly sales reports for seven stores and four products from POS exports.
- Find and explain changes in sales: promotions, stockouts, seasonal patterns.
- Prepare short briefs for the regional sales manager.

## Required
- Strong Excel: PivotTables, lookups, and charts.
- Clear written communication.
- Able to start by January 5, 2027.

## Preferred
- A bachelor's degree in business, economics, or a related field.
- Experience with retail or sales data (internship, job, or project).
- Some experience with SQL, Python, or Power BI.
